# RiVal: project hub

RiVal is an independent watch brand in the making, by an Estonian father-and-son team: an engineer (product development and manufacturing) and a goldsmith (casting, finishing). It has a network of CNC and hand-engraving partners in Latvia.

**Model:** we design and engineer → outsource parts to low-MOQ specialists → assemble, finish and engrave by hand → launch on Kickstarter with a community built beforehand on Instagram ([@rival_watches](https://www.instagram.com/rival_watches)).

**Philosophy:** take accessible, proven movements (Seagull, Miyota, Sellita, ETA) and turn them into avant-garde horology in the spirit of Urwerk, MB&F and Jacob & Co.

## Team & assets

- **Engineering / product / supply chain:** Tallinn. BSc mechanical engineering (product development & manufacturing, TalTech 2019), OEM account management at SKF.
- **Goldsmith workshop:** Kuressaare. Sketch → CAD → CNC wax → lost-wax casting → finishing. Track A watch (bronze case, ETA) already built; Seagull/Miyota movements and a diver case on hand.
- **Partner, Latvia:** CNC machining, CNC and manual hand engraving (cases, guns, flasks, trophies).
- **Tools:** full watchmaker tool sets (2x). Movements on hand: _to inventory, see ROADMAP_.

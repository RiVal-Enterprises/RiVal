## Idea in one sentence
Twelve hour "planets" ring the dial; only the current hour turns its numeral toward you, while a small rocket orbits the dial as the minute hand.

## How it shows time
A toothed sector under the planets steps 30° each hour and turns two planets 180° via bevel pinions: the passing hour turns away, the new hour turns in. Minutes: rocket on a ring geared 1:1 to the centre arbor.

## Why it's special
Reads like a solar system; theatrical hour change; can be made from cast precious metal (family goldsmith workshop) and hand-engraved (Latvia).

## Geneva timing reference

| Slots | Engaged angle | Duration if driver turns 1x/hour |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | 90 deg | 15.0 min |
| 6 | 120 deg | 20.0 min |
| 8 | 135 deg | 22.5 min |
| 12 | 150 deg | 25.0 min |

A Geneva driven once per hour can never give a crisp jump; it needs either a fast driver with a reduction after it, or (recommended) a snail cam + jumper that stores energy over the hour.

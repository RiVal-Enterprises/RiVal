# Barrelhand · Project 1 · 2017-2020

- Founder: Karel Bachand, mechanical-engineering student (San Jose State), San Francisco
- Origin: ~2 years building a working 3D-printed Urwerk UR-202 homage (Miyota base, machined Al plate, multijet-printed parts) → Urwerk founders invited him to SIHH 2014
- Project 1: heavily modified Eterna 3901M, linear minute display, Geneva-driven intermittent hour, DMLS metal parts; small limited edition (20 pcs initial form; numbers vary by source)
- Later: Monolith, case printed in Scalmalloy (6.5 g)
- What they did right: a public, credible prototype; leaned on additive manufacturing; accessible base movement
- Lesson for RiVal: rules 1-3

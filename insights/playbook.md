# RiVal playbook (rules from evidence)

Confidence: ★ = one case, ★★ = several cases, ★★★ = consistent pattern. Rules start as hypotheses and get promoted or dropped as cases accumulate.

## Product
1. **Proven movement + original display/case is a viable path to "high-horology" perception.** ★ (Barrelhand Project 1: modified Eterna 3901M + Geneva hour + printed metal case)
2. **Expect years, not months, from first prototype to product.** ★ (Barrelhand: about 2 years to the homage, about 6 years to Project 1)

## Community & launch
3. **A credible working prototype gets the industry's attention.** ★ (Bachand's UR-202 homage → invited by Urwerk to SIHH 2014)
4. **Build the audience before the campaign; most crowdfunding success comes from the pre-launch list.** (hypothesis, to be tested with campaign data)

## Supply chain
5. **Prototype with 1-pc services; commit to MOQ only after the campaign.** (hypothesis)

## Failure modes to avoid
_(filled by autopsies: delivery delays, underpriced rewards, movement shortages, quality complaints, scope creep...)_

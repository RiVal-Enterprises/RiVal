# Supplier research log

## 2026-11-24 · First pass (interactive session)

**Found:** 21 external leads (S001-S021) across parts houses, movements, Chinese OEM, on-demand CNC/3D printing, micro-SLA, LIGA and micro metal printing. Plus the two in-house/family partners.

**Key takeaways**
- 1-piece is realistic for almost everything except custom modules: parts houses (Cousins, Boley) for movements and components; JLC3DP/PCBWay for 1-pc CNC and metal prints (user reports: brass CNC case ~US$45, 316L printed case ~US$16); IPFL/BANTLE for 2 µm micro-SLA gears.
- Custom complication parts at watch precision: UV-LIGA (Mimotec) is the Swiss route for gears/springs in small batches.
- Wire EDM is limited for very fine watch gear teeth (wire too thick for tooth-tip radii); micro-EDM / LIGA preferred for small modules.
- Chinese jump-hour / wandering-hour (8215-based) movements exist with MOQ 300-500. Ask for paid samples as teardown references.
- Seagull ST3600: ~US$38-50 single (eBay), ~US$25 at 100+ (Alibaba).
